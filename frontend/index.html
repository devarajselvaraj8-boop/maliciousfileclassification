⚠️ False Negative (FN) — Missed Malware

Definition: A malicious file incorrectly classified as benign.

Why It Matters: This is the most dangerous failure mode in cybersecurity. A false negative allows active ransomware, trojans, or spyware to bypass defenses, execute on the host, and compromise the network.

⚠️ False Positive (FP) — False Alarm

Definition: A benign file incorrectly classified as malicious.

Why It Matters: Leads to analyst alert fatigue and business disruptions when legitimate software is quarantined for investigation.
